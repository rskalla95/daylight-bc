Our Services

Supporting mental health providers with comprehensive billing, financial, and credentialing solutions—so you can focus on what matters most: caring for your clients.

Behavioral Health Billing

We provide comprehensive insurance billing services tailored specifically for mental health providers. From accurate claims submission to timely follow-ups, we manage the entire revenue cycle to ensure maximum reimbursement for your practice. Our services include insurance verification, denial management, and appeals to recover lost revenue. We proactively track claims, resolve rejections, and minimize delays in payment.

Financial Consulting

We provide strategic financial consulting to help your practice thrive. From budgeting and revenue optimization to cash flow management, we deliver the insights you need to make informed decisions. Our services include financial analysis, detailed reporting, forecasting, bookkeeping, and expense tracking to give you a clear picture of your practice's financial health and identify growth opportunities.

Credentialing

We offer credentialing services for mental health providers, including CAQH management, tracking renewals, initiating new applications, and negotiating higher reimbursement rates when applicable. We manage the entire credentialing process from start to finish, ensuring accuracy, compliance, and timely approvals with insurance payers.
