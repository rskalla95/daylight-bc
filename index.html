Our Services

Supporting mental health providers with comprehensive billing, financial, and credentialing solutions—so you can focus on what matters most: caring for your clients.

Behavioral Health Billing

  • Comprehensive insurance billing tailored for mental health providers
  • Accurate claims submission and timely follow-ups
  • Complete revenue cycle management
  • Insurance verification and eligibility checks
  • Denial management and appeals to recover lost revenue
  • Proactive claim tracking to minimize payment delays

Financial Consulting

  • Strategic budgeting and revenue optimization
  • Cash flow management and analysis
  • Detailed financial reporting and forecasting
  • Bookkeeping and expense tracking
  • Growth opportunity identification
  • Clear insights into your practice's financial health

Credentialing

  • Credential renewal tracking and reminders
  • New application initiation and processing
  • Reimbursement rate negotiation when applicable
  • End-to-end credentialing process management
  • Accuracy, compliance, and timely insurance approvals
  • CAQH profile management and maintenance

Meet Kelsie & Brad Skalla

Kelsie founded Daylight Billing & Consulting in 2023 with a vision to help mental health providers spend less time on paperwork and more time doing what they love—caring for their patients. With over six years of experience in mental health billing, credentialing, and operations, she brings a deep understanding of the unique challenges providers face.

Recently, Brad joined the team to help grow the business and bring his financial expertise to support clients even further. With his MBA from Utah State University and 7 years in corporate finance, and Kelsie's industry experience, together they're building a service that truly understands your needs.

Whether it's navigating insurance complexities or optimizing your practice's financial health, they're here to help you thrive.
